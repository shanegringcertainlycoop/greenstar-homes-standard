# Certification levels

Requirements are additive: each level includes every requirement of the levels below it.

## Level 1: Certified
The entry GreenStar tier. Covers mandatory safety, water, energy, and material assessments that every certified project must complete.

## Level 2: Silver
Silver requires all Certified requirements plus tighter water/energy targets, improved filtration, basic ventilation upgrades, and documented occupant training.

## Level 3: Gold
Gold requires all Certified + Silver requirements, plus electrification of major appliances, balanced ventilation, in-home air quality sensing, and a 15% embodied-carbon reduction in new construction.

## Level 4: Platinum
Platinum requires all lower-tier requirements plus full electrification, heat- or energy-recovery ventilation, humidity control, real-time water leak detection, solar/EV readiness, and a 30% embodied-carbon reduction in new construction.
