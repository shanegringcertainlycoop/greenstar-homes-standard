# Pillar 3: Water Efficiency & Management

Cut indoor and outdoor water use without sacrificing function. Covers fixture flow rates, leak prevention, plumbing pressure testing, and (at Platinum) real-time leak detection.

## Showerhead Flow Rate
*Required at: Certified level and above*

Average measured flow rate of all showerheads in the home. Alternative pathway: meet a Home Water Score threshold.

**Scope:**

All showerheads installed in the dwelling unit, including primary, guest, and accessory bathrooms. Hand-held wands count as part of the fixture they share a valve with. Bathtub spouts and tub-only fillers are excluded.

**By level:**

- Certified: ≤ 2.2 GPM average — or Home Water Score of 2
- Silver: ≤ 2.0 GPM average — or Home Water Score of 3
- Gold: ≤ 1.5 GPM average — or Home Water Score of 5
- Platinum: ≤ 1.25 GPM average — or Home Water Score of 6

*Counts toward badges: zero-water-capable, home-water-score*

Reference: https://standard.greenstarhomes.org/requirements/showerhead-flow-rate

## Bathroom Aerator Flow Rate
*Required at: Certified level and above*

Average measured flow rate of all bathroom faucet aerators.

**Scope:**

Aerators on every bathroom lavatory (sink) faucet in the dwelling. Kitchen, utility, and laundry-sink aerators are excluded.

**By level:**

- Certified: ≤ 2.0 GPM average — or Home Water Score of 2
- Silver: ≤ 1.75 GPM average — or Home Water Score of 3
- Gold: ≤ 1.0 GPM average — or Home Water Score of 5
- Platinum: ≤ 0.5 GPM average — or Home Water Score of 6

*Counts toward badges: zero-water-capable, home-water-score*

Reference: https://standard.greenstarhomes.org/requirements/bathroom-aerator-flow-rate

## Toilet Flush Volume
*Required at: Certified level and above*

Average flush volume across all toilets in the home.

**Scope:**

Every toilet installed in the dwelling unit, including dual-flush models. Bathtubs, urinals (residential only), and bidets are excluded.

**By level:**

- Certified: < 1.6 GPF average — or Home Water Score of 2
- Silver: < 1.3 GPF average — or Home Water Score of 3
- Gold: ≤ 1.0 GPF average — or Home Water Score of 5
- Platinum: < 1.0 GPF average — or Home Water Score of 6

*Counts toward badges: zero-water-capable, home-water-score*

Reference: https://standard.greenstarhomes.org/requirements/toilet-flush-volume

## Plumbing Leak & Pressure Test
*Required at: Certified level and above*

Pressure test the plumbing system; no leaks permitted. Documented via the LEED-format Water Pressure and Leak Verification Report.

**Scope:**

All cold- and hot-water supply piping inside the dwelling envelope, from the point of entry (meter or well pressure tank) through every fixture connection.

*Counts toward badges: zero-water-capable, home-water-score*

Reference: https://standard.greenstarhomes.org/requirements/plumbing-leak-pressure-test

## Clothes Washer Leak Protection
*Required at: Certified level and above*

Use steel-braided supply hoses, OR install a drain pan under the clothes washer.

**Scope:**

Every clothes washer installation in the dwelling, including stacked or laundry-closet units.

*Counts toward badges: home-water-score*

Reference: https://standard.greenstarhomes.org/requirements/washer-leak-protection

## Tank Water Heater Drain Pan
*Required at: Certified level and above*

Install a drain pan under any tank water heater located in or above conditioned living space.

**Scope:**

Tank-style water heaters located in or above conditioned living space (e.g. utility closets on upper floors, attic installations). Garage and basement installations are excluded if a tank failure would not damage living space.

Reference: https://standard.greenstarhomes.org/requirements/water-heater-drain-pan

## Cold-Water Pipe Insulation
*Required at: Gold level and above*

Insulate cold-water pipes, or keep them out of unconditioned space.

**Scope:**

Cold-water supply lines running through any unconditioned space (attics, crawlspaces, exterior walls in cold climates, garages, vented soffits).

*Counts toward badges: zero-water-capable*

Reference: https://standard.greenstarhomes.org/requirements/cold-water-pipe-insulation

## Tap Water Filtration
*Required at: Gold level and above*

Install tap-water filtration appropriate to local water-quality conditions.

**Scope:**

The primary drinking-water tap (typically the kitchen sink cold tap). Whole-house filtration is acceptable but not required.

*Counts toward badges: zero-water-capable*

Reference: https://standard.greenstarhomes.org/requirements/tap-water-filtration

## On-Demand Well-Water Softener
*Required at: Gold level and above*

Where well water is the supply, use an on-demand softener.

**Scope:**

Dwellings on well water with measured hardness above 7 grains per gallon (gpg) or 120 mg/L as CaCO₃.

Reference: https://standard.greenstarhomes.org/requirements/well-water-on-demand-softener

## Advanced Hot Water Delivery Test
*Required at: Platinum level and above*

Hot-water delivery passes the advanced test: ≤ 0.6 gallons before delivery and +10°F temperature rise.

**Scope:**

Hot-water distribution from the water heater (or recirculation manifold) to the farthest hot-water fixture in the home.

*Counts toward badges: home-water-score*

Reference: https://standard.greenstarhomes.org/requirements/advanced-plumbing-test

## Water Leak Detection & Real-Time Metering
*Required at: Platinum level and above*

Real-time water leak detection and metering system installed.

**Scope:**

Whole-home plumbing — the system must monitor the entire supply line for unusual flow patterns indicative of a leak.

*Counts toward badges: zero-water-capable, resilience, home-water-score*

Reference: https://standard.greenstarhomes.org/requirements/water-leak-detection-metering
