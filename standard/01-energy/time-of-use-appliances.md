# Time-of-Use / Peak-Load Appliances

*Pillar: Energy & Climate Resilience · Required at: Platinum level and above*

Major appliances support time-of-use scheduling and/or peak-load avoidance.

**Scope:**

Major-load appliances: HVAC, water heater, EV charger, electric dryer, dishwasher, and (where present) battery storage system.

*Counts toward badges: resilience, peak-load-shaver*
