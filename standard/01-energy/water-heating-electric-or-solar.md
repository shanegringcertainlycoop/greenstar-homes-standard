# All-Electric or Solar Water Heating

*Pillar: Energy & Climate Resilience · Required at: Gold level and above*

Water heating is all-electric (heat pump preferred) or solar.

**Scope:**

The primary domestic hot-water heater serving the dwelling. Recirculation loops and point-of-use boosters are downstream of this and not constrained by this requirement.

*Counts toward badges: ghi-zero-energy-certified, ghi-zero-carbon-certified, ghi-zero-energy-ready, electrified-living, peak-load-shaver*
