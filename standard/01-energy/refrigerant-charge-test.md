# Refrigerant Charge Verification

*Pillar: Energy & Climate Resilience · Required at: Silver level and above*

Refrigerant charge test completed on all AC and heat-pump equipment.

**Scope:**

All split-system AC and heat-pump equipment installed (factory-charged window/PTAC units excluded).
