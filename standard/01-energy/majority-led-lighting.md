# Majority LED Lighting

*Pillar: Energy & Climate Resilience · Required at: Certified level and above*

A majority of fixtures use LED lamps.

**Scope:**

All interior and exterior lighting fixtures in the dwelling. Decorative-only fixtures (e.g. chandeliers used <1 hr/day) count toward the total.

*Counts toward badges: ghi-zero-energy-certified, ghi-zero-energy-ready, reduced-emf*
