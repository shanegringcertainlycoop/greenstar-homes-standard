# Climate Risk Assessment

*Pillar: Energy & Climate Resilience · Required at: Certified level and above*

Determine the project's future climate risks using a climate-resource tool. Document and mitigate identified risks (stringency rises by tier).

**Scope:**

Site-specific climate risks over the project's expected lifetime (typically 30-60 years), identified using a recognized climate-projection tool.

*Counts toward badges: resilience, nature-friendly, walkability*
