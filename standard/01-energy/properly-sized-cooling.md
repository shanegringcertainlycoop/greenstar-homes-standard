# Properly Sized Cooling Equipment

*Pillar: Energy & Climate Resilience · Required at: Silver level and above*

Cooling equipment sized per ACCA Manual J 8th Edition (or equivalent load calculation).

**Scope:**

All air-conditioning and heat-pump equipment installed for space cooling — central, mini-split, and packaged systems.
