# Solar- and EV-Ready Electrical Service

*Pillar: Energy & Climate Resilience · Required at: Platinum level and above*

200-amp breaker box with capacity to add future solar PV and electric-vehicle chargers.

**Scope:**

The main electrical service panel and the conduit/wiring runs from panel to roof and panel to the EV-charging location.

*Counts toward badges: ghi-zero-energy-certified, ghi-zero-carbon-certified, ghi-zero-energy-ready, resilience, walkability, peak-load-shaver*
