# All-Electric or Dual-Fuel Heating

*Pillar: Energy & Climate Resilience · Required at: Gold level and above*

Space heating is all-electric or dual-fuel/hybrid. (At Platinum, all-electric only.)

**Scope:**

The primary space-heating system — central heat pump, ducted air handler, mini-split, or hydronic system.

*Counts toward badges: ghi-zero-energy-certified, ghi-zero-carbon-certified, ghi-zero-energy-ready, electrified-living, peak-load-shaver*
