# Air Leakage Between Attached Units

*Pillar: Energy & Climate Resilience · Required at: Certified level and above*

Reduce air movement between attached housing units. Stringency tightens at higher tiers (ACH50 thresholds differ for new construction vs. renovation).

**Scope:**

Air leakage between adjoining dwelling units in attached housing — duplex, townhouse, multifamily, ADU sharing a wall. Single-family detached homes are exempt.

**By level:**

- Certified: Reduce air leaks between attached units (no specific threshold)
- Silver: 0.3 ACH50 (new) / 0.4 (existing) between units
- Gold: 0.23 ACH50 (new) / 0.3 (renovation) between units
- Platinum: 0.15 ACH50 (new) / 0.23 (renovation) between units
