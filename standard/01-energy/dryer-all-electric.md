# All-Electric Dryer

*Pillar: Energy & Climate Resilience · Required at: Gold level and above*

Clothes dryer is all-electric (heat-pump dryer preferred).

**Scope:**

The primary clothes dryer in the dwelling.

*Counts toward badges: ghi-zero-energy-certified, ghi-zero-carbon-certified, ghi-zero-energy-ready, electrified-living, peak-load-shaver*
