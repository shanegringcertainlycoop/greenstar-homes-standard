# Building Maintenance Instructions

*Pillar: Materials & Environmental Impact · Required at: Silver level and above*

Create written instructions documenting how to maintain the building, equipment, and finishes.

**Scope:**

Written documentation of how to maintain the building, equipment, and finishes — handed off to the homeowner or tenant at project completion.

*Counts toward badges: accessibility, resilience*
