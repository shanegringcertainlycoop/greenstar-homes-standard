# Certified Wood (No Tropical Species)

*Pillar: Materials & Environmental Impact · Required at: Gold level and above · new construction only*

New wood products are FSC or SFI certified. No tropical wood.

**Scope:**

All new wood products installed in the dwelling — framing, flooring, trim, cabinetry, decking, structural panels.

*Counts toward badges: sustainable-materials*
