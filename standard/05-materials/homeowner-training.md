# Homeowner / Tenant Training

*Pillar: Materials & Environmental Impact · Required at: Silver level and above*

Train the homeowner or tenant on how to operate and maintain the building.

**Scope:**

In-person walkthrough and training session with the homeowner or tenant at project handover.

*Counts toward badges: accessibility, resilience*
