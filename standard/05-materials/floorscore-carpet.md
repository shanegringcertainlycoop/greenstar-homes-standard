# Floorscore or Green Label Plus Carpet

*Pillar: Materials & Environmental Impact · Required at: Platinum level and above · new construction only*

Any new carpet is Floorscore or Green Label Plus certified.

**Scope:**

Any newly installed carpet in the dwelling. Existing carpet that is being kept in place is exempt.

*Counts toward badges: sustainable-materials*
