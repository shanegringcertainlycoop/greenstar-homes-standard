# Asbestos Mitigation

*Pillar: Materials & Environmental Impact · Required at: Certified level and above · existing buildings only*

Mitigate any asbestos found, or document that none is present.

**Scope:**

Asbestos-containing materials in existing dwellings, typically built before the U.S. asbestos phase-out (1989-current, depending on product).

*Counts toward badges: sustainable-materials*
