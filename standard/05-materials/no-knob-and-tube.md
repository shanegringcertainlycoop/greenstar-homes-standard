# Knob-and-Tube Wiring Removed

*Pillar: Materials & Environmental Impact · Required at: Certified level and above · existing buildings only*

No knob-and-tube wiring remains active in the home.

**Scope:**

Knob-and-tube electrical wiring in existing dwellings (common in U.S. homes built 1880-1940).

*Counts toward badges: reduced-emf*
