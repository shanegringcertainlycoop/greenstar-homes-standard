# Range Hood Tested at 100 CFM (or Electric Stove)

*Pillar: Indoor Air Quality & Health · Required at: Certified level and above*

Either install an electric stove, or have a range hood that is tested to vent at least 100 CFM to the outdoors.

**Scope:**

The kitchen cooking surface. Choose one of the two compliance paths — electric stove (eliminates combustion) or vented range hood (removes combustion products if gas is used).

*Counts toward badges: healthier-home*
