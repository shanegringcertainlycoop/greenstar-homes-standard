# Ventilation with Heat/Energy Recovery or Smart Controls

*Pillar: Indoor Air Quality & Health · Required at: Platinum level and above*

Ventilation includes energy-recovery / heat-recovery (ERV/HRV) or smart-control features.

**Scope:**

The whole-house ventilation system, building on the Silver and Gold ventilation requirements.

*Counts toward badges: healthier-home*
