# No Indoor Combustion Fireplace

*Pillar: Indoor Air Quality & Health · Required at: Platinum level and above*

No combustion fireplaces present indoors. Electric fireplaces permitted.

**Scope:**

Any indoor combustion-based fireplace (wood, gas, pellet, ethanol).
