# Gas Leak Inspection

*Pillar: Indoor Air Quality & Health · Required at: Certified level and above*

All gas appliances and piping inspected for leaks. No leaks permitted; repair or replace before certification.

**Scope:**

All natural-gas and propane plumbing inside the dwelling — supply piping, fittings, appliance connections, and shutoff valves.
