# HVAC Filter Rating

*Pillar: Indoor Air Quality & Health · Required at: Certified level and above*

Minimum MERV rating for filters on ducted heating/cooling systems.

**Scope:**

All HVAC return-air filters in ducted heating, cooling, and ventilation systems serving the dwelling.

**By level:**

- Certified: MERV 8 minimum
- Silver: MERV 10 minimum
- Gold: MERV 13 minimum
- Platinum: MERV 16 minimum

*Counts toward badges: healthier-home*
