# Radon Testing and Mitigation

*Pillar: Indoor Air Quality & Health · Required at: Certified level and above*

Test for radon; no levels above PCL 4 permitted. Install a passive or active mitigation system if needed. All new construction and all EPA Zone 1 homes require a system.

**Scope:**

Indoor radon levels throughout the dwelling, with emphasis on the lowest livable floor.

*Counts toward badges: healthier-home*
