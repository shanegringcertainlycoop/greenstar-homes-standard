# Moisture-Resistant Flooring in Wet Areas

*Pillar: Moisture & Structural Integrity · Required at: Silver level and above*

Bathrooms, kitchens, main entryways, and laundry rooms use moisture-resistant flooring.

**Scope:**

Floor finishes in wet areas: bathrooms, kitchens, main entryways, and laundry rooms.
