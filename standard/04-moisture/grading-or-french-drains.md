# Site Grading or French Drains

*Pillar: Moisture & Structural Integrity · Required at: Silver level and above · new construction only*

Grading slopes away from the home — or french drains are installed (new construction).

**Scope:**

Site grading and subsurface drainage in new construction projects.

*Counts toward badges: nature-friendly, stormwater-control*
