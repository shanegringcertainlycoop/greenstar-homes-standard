# No New Ducts Panned in Joists

*Pillar: Moisture & Structural Integrity · Required at: Silver level and above · new construction only*

No newly installed ducts are panned in floor joists — they must be fully ducted to prevent unintended air paths.

**Scope:**

Newly installed HVAC return-air ductwork in new construction.
