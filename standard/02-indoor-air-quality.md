# Pillar 2: Indoor Air Quality & Health

Keep occupants safe from combustion gases, radon, mold, and indoor pollutants. Covers gas/CO testing, MERV filtration, radon mitigation, kitchen/bath exhaust, and humidity control.

## HVAC Filter Rating
*Required at: Certified level and above*

Minimum MERV rating for filters on ducted heating/cooling systems.

**Scope:**

All HVAC return-air filters in ducted heating, cooling, and ventilation systems serving the dwelling.

**By level:**

- Certified: MERV 8 minimum
- Silver: MERV 10 minimum
- Gold: MERV 13 minimum
- Platinum: MERV 16 minimum

*Counts toward badges: healthier-home*

Reference: https://standard.greenstarhomes.org/requirements/hvac-filter-rating

## Bathroom Exhaust Ventilation
*Required at: Certified level and above*

Bath fans exhaust to the outdoors (verified with a toilet-paper test). Silver and above must also test to ≥ 50 CFM per bathroom.

**Scope:**

Exhaust fans in every bathroom — full bath, half-bath, and powder rooms with a permanent water source.

*Counts toward badges: healthier-home*

Reference: https://standard.greenstarhomes.org/requirements/bath-fan-exhaust

## Range Hood Tested at 100 CFM (or Electric Stove)
*Required at: Certified level and above*

Either install an electric stove, or have a range hood that is tested to vent at least 100 CFM to the outdoors.

**Scope:**

The kitchen cooking surface. Choose one of the two compliance paths — electric stove (eliminates combustion) or vented range hood (removes combustion products if gas is used).

*Counts toward badges: healthier-home*

Reference: https://standard.greenstarhomes.org/requirements/range-hood-or-electric-stove

## Gas Leak Inspection
*Required at: Certified level and above*

All gas appliances and piping inspected for leaks. No leaks permitted; repair or replace before certification.

**Scope:**

All natural-gas and propane plumbing inside the dwelling — supply piping, fittings, appliance connections, and shutoff valves.

Reference: https://standard.greenstarhomes.org/requirements/gas-leak-inspection

## Carbon Monoxide Testing of Combustion Appliances
*Required at: Certified level and above*

CO leak test performed on all combustion-based appliances. Repair or replace any appliance that fails.

**Scope:**

All combustion-based appliances in the dwelling — furnace, boiler, water heater, gas range/oven, gas dryer, and gas fireplace.

Reference: https://standard.greenstarhomes.org/requirements/combustion-co-test

## Carbon Monoxide Detectors
*Required at: Certified level and above*

Hardwired CO detectors installed on every floor and near any fireplace.

**Scope:**

Carbon-monoxide detection throughout the dwelling.

Reference: https://standard.greenstarhomes.org/requirements/co-detectors

## Radon Testing and Mitigation
*Required at: Certified level and above*

Test for radon; no levels above PCL 4 permitted. Install a passive or active mitigation system if needed. All new construction and all EPA Zone 1 homes require a system.

**Scope:**

Indoor radon levels throughout the dwelling, with emphasis on the lowest livable floor.

*Counts toward badges: healthier-home*

Reference: https://standard.greenstarhomes.org/requirements/radon-mitigation

## Fireplace Safety
*Required at: Certified level and above*

If a combustion fireplace exists: seal, vent, provide makeup air, and install a CO detector. (At Platinum, no combustion fireplaces indoors — electric only.)

**Scope:**

Any combustion-based fireplace (wood, gas, pellet) installed in the dwelling — both new and existing.

Reference: https://standard.greenstarhomes.org/requirements/fireplace-safety

## Whole-House Fresh Air Supply
*Required at: Silver level and above*

A whole-house mechanical fresh-air supply system is provided.

**Scope:**

Continuous or intermittent mechanical ventilation that supplies outdoor air to the dwelling — separate from spot ventilation (bath fans, kitchen hood).

*Counts toward badges: healthier-home*

Reference: https://standard.greenstarhomes.org/requirements/whole-house-fresh-air

## Sealed/Vented New Gas Appliances
*Required at: Silver level and above*

Any new gas appliances are sealed-combustion / vented to the outdoors — or the project goes all-electric.

**Scope:**

All NEW gas appliances installed in the dwelling — furnace, boiler, water heater, dryer, fireplace.

Reference: https://standard.greenstarhomes.org/requirements/sealed-vented-gas-appliances

## Kitchen Ventilation
*Required at: Silver level and above*

Kitchen has proper ventilation via range hood, outdoor air system, or air-quality monitor. (At Platinum, the range hood must detect PM2.5 and activate automatically.)

**Scope:**

Kitchen ventilation supplementing or extending the basic range hood requirement.

*Counts toward badges: healthier-home*

Reference: https://standard.greenstarhomes.org/requirements/kitchen-ventilation

## Balanced Fresh-Air System
*Required at: Gold level and above*

A balanced (supply + exhaust) fresh-air system is installed and tested.

**Scope:**

Mechanical ventilation system delivering balanced supply and exhaust airflow — HRV (heat recovery ventilator) or ERV (energy recovery ventilator).

*Counts toward badges: healthier-home*

Reference: https://standard.greenstarhomes.org/requirements/balanced-fresh-air-system

## Indoor Air Quality Sensor
*Required at: Gold level and above*

RESET-certified air-quality sensor installed in the main living area or ducts. (At Platinum, sensor measures CO₂, TVOC, humidity, temp, and PM2.5.)

**Scope:**

Continuous indoor air-quality monitoring in the dwelling.

*Counts toward badges: healthier-home, reduced-emf*

Reference: https://standard.greenstarhomes.org/requirements/air-quality-sensor

## Ventilation with Heat/Energy Recovery or Smart Controls
*Required at: Platinum level and above*

Ventilation includes energy-recovery / heat-recovery (ERV/HRV) or smart-control features.

**Scope:**

The whole-house ventilation system, building on the Silver and Gold ventilation requirements.

*Counts toward badges: healthier-home*

Reference: https://standard.greenstarhomes.org/requirements/ventilation-heat-recovery

## Whole-Home Humidity Control
*Required at: Platinum level and above*

Whole-home dehumidification / humidification provided where appropriate by climate (target 40–50°F dewpoint).

**Scope:**

Whole-home humidity management — dehumidification in cooling season, humidification in heating season, as appropriate to climate.

*Counts toward badges: healthier-home*

Reference: https://standard.greenstarhomes.org/requirements/humidity-control

## No Indoor Combustion Fireplace
*Required at: Platinum level and above*

No combustion fireplaces present indoors. Electric fireplaces permitted.

**Scope:**

Any indoor combustion-based fireplace (wood, gas, pellet, ethanol).

Reference: https://standard.greenstarhomes.org/requirements/no-combustion-fireplace
