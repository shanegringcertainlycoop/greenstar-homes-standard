# On-Demand Well-Water Softener

*Pillar: Water Efficiency & Management · Required at: Gold level and above*

Where well water is the supply, use an on-demand softener.

**Scope:**

Dwellings on well water with measured hardness above 7 grains per gallon (gpg) or 120 mg/L as CaCO₃.
