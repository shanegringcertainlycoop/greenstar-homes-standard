# Cold-Water Pipe Insulation

*Pillar: Water Efficiency & Management · Required at: Gold level and above*

Insulate cold-water pipes, or keep them out of unconditioned space.

**Scope:**

Cold-water supply lines running through any unconditioned space (attics, crawlspaces, exterior walls in cold climates, garages, vented soffits).

*Counts toward badges: zero-water-capable*
