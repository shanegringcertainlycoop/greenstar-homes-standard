# Showerhead Flow Rate

*Pillar: Water Efficiency & Management · Required at: Certified level and above*

Average measured flow rate of all showerheads in the home. Alternative pathway: meet a Home Water Score threshold.

**Scope:**

All showerheads installed in the dwelling unit, including primary, guest, and accessory bathrooms. Hand-held wands count as part of the fixture they share a valve with. Bathtub spouts and tub-only fillers are excluded.

**By level:**

- Certified: ≤ 2.2 GPM average — or Home Water Score of 2
- Silver: ≤ 2.0 GPM average — or Home Water Score of 3
- Gold: ≤ 1.5 GPM average — or Home Water Score of 5
- Platinum: ≤ 1.25 GPM average — or Home Water Score of 6

*Counts toward badges: zero-water-capable, home-water-score*
