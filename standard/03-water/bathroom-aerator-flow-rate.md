# Bathroom Aerator Flow Rate

*Pillar: Water Efficiency & Management · Required at: Certified level and above*

Average measured flow rate of all bathroom faucet aerators.

**Scope:**

Aerators on every bathroom lavatory (sink) faucet in the dwelling. Kitchen, utility, and laundry-sink aerators are excluded.

**By level:**

- Certified: ≤ 2.0 GPM average — or Home Water Score of 2
- Silver: ≤ 1.75 GPM average — or Home Water Score of 3
- Gold: ≤ 1.0 GPM average — or Home Water Score of 5
- Platinum: ≤ 0.5 GPM average — or Home Water Score of 6

*Counts toward badges: zero-water-capable, home-water-score*
