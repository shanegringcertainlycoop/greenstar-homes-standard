# Toilet Flush Volume

*Pillar: Water Efficiency & Management · Required at: Certified level and above*

Average flush volume across all toilets in the home.

**Scope:**

Every toilet installed in the dwelling unit, including dual-flush models. Bathtubs, urinals (residential only), and bidets are excluded.

**By level:**

- Certified: < 1.6 GPF average — or Home Water Score of 2
- Silver: < 1.3 GPF average — or Home Water Score of 3
- Gold: ≤ 1.0 GPF average — or Home Water Score of 5
- Platinum: < 1.0 GPF average — or Home Water Score of 6

*Counts toward badges: zero-water-capable, home-water-score*
