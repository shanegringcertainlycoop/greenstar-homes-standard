# Clothes Washer Leak Protection

*Pillar: Water Efficiency & Management · Required at: Certified level and above*

Use steel-braided supply hoses, OR install a drain pan under the clothes washer.

**Scope:**

Every clothes washer installation in the dwelling, including stacked or laundry-closet units.

*Counts toward badges: home-water-score*
