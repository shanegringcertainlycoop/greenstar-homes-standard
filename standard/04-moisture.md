# Pillar 4: Moisture & Structural Integrity

Keep water out of the building envelope and shed it correctly at the site. Covers moisture assessments, gutter and grading systems, certified wet-area assemblies, and duct quality.

## Moisture & Wall Water Leak Assessment
*Required at: Certified level and above*

Inspect roof condition, flashing, gutters, siding, ground slope, basement moisture (meter), and visible mold/mildew. Repair findings.

**Scope:**

The entire dwelling envelope and grounds — roof, flashing, gutters, siding, foundation, basement/crawlspace, around plumbing penetrations, and visible interior surfaces.

*Counts toward badges: nature-friendly, stormwater-control*

Reference: https://standard.greenstarhomes.org/requirements/moisture-water-leak-assessment

## Functional Gutter System
*Required at: Certified level and above*

Gutters drain water well away from the foundation — OR site has well-draining sandy soils verified per USDA classification.

**Scope:**

Roof drainage system — gutters, downspouts, splash blocks, and the ground area where roof water terminates.

*Counts toward badges: nature-friendly, stormwater-control*

Reference: https://standard.greenstarhomes.org/requirements/gutter-system

## Site Grading or French Drains
*Required at: Silver level and above · new construction only*

Grading slopes away from the home — or french drains are installed (new construction).

**Scope:**

Site grading and subsurface drainage in new construction projects.

*Counts toward badges: nature-friendly, stormwater-control*

Reference: https://standard.greenstarhomes.org/requirements/grading-or-french-drains

## No New Ducts Panned in Joists
*Required at: Silver level and above · new construction only*

No newly installed ducts are panned in floor joists — they must be fully ducted to prevent unintended air paths.

**Scope:**

Newly installed HVAC return-air ductwork in new construction.

Reference: https://standard.greenstarhomes.org/requirements/no-new-ducts-panned-in-joists

## Moisture-Resistant Flooring in Wet Areas
*Required at: Silver level and above*

Bathrooms, kitchens, main entryways, and laundry rooms use moisture-resistant flooring.

**Scope:**

Floor finishes in wet areas: bathrooms, kitchens, main entryways, and laundry rooms.

Reference: https://standard.greenstarhomes.org/requirements/moisture-resistant-flooring

## Certified Shower/Tub Surrounds
*Required at: Silver level and above*

New shower/tub surrounds use ASTM D3273-certified wallboard, certified coating, or drywall-free construction.

**Scope:**

Wall surfaces inside shower and bathtub surrounds — the wet zones behind tile, panels, or solid surface.

Reference: https://standard.greenstarhomes.org/requirements/certified-shower-tub-surrounds

## All Ducts Fully Ducted
*Required at: Platinum level and above*

All HVAC ducts are fully ducted — none panned in joists.

**Scope:**

All HVAC supply and return ductwork in the dwelling — new and existing.

Reference: https://standard.greenstarhomes.org/requirements/all-ducts-fully-ducted
