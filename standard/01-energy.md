# Pillar 1: Energy & Climate Resilience

Reduce a home's energy use, electrify its mechanical systems, and harden it against future climate risks. Covers Home Energy Score, heating/cooling, water heating, cooking, ventilation, and air-leakage targets.

## Climate Risk Assessment
*Required at: Certified level and above*

Determine the project's future climate risks using a climate-resource tool. Document and mitigate identified risks (stringency rises by tier).

**Scope:**

Site-specific climate risks over the project's expected lifetime (typically 30-60 years), identified using a recognized climate-projection tool.

*Counts toward badges: resilience, nature-friendly, walkability*

Reference: https://standard.greenstarhomes.org/requirements/climate-risk-assessment

## Majority LED Lighting
*Required at: Certified level and above*

A majority of fixtures use LED lamps.

**Scope:**

All interior and exterior lighting fixtures in the dwelling. Decorative-only fixtures (e.g. chandeliers used <1 hr/day) count toward the total.

*Counts toward badges: ghi-zero-energy-certified, ghi-zero-energy-ready, reduced-emf*

Reference: https://standard.greenstarhomes.org/requirements/majority-led-lighting

## Air Leakage Between Attached Units
*Required at: Certified level and above*

Reduce air movement between attached housing units. Stringency tightens at higher tiers (ACH50 thresholds differ for new construction vs. renovation).

**Scope:**

Air leakage between adjoining dwelling units in attached housing — duplex, townhouse, multifamily, ADU sharing a wall. Single-family detached homes are exempt.

**By level:**

- Certified: Reduce air leaks between attached units (no specific threshold)
- Silver: 0.3 ACH50 (new) / 0.4 (existing) between units
- Gold: 0.23 ACH50 (new) / 0.3 (renovation) between units
- Platinum: 0.15 ACH50 (new) / 0.23 (renovation) between units

Reference: https://standard.greenstarhomes.org/requirements/attached-housing-air-leakage

## Home Energy Performance
*Required at: Silver level and above*

DOE Home Energy Score (existing) or modeled annual energy use (new construction). Alternative tools include HERS, PHPP, WUFI Passive, BeOpt, and 90.1.

**Scope:**

Whole-house energy performance, rated using DOE Home Energy Score or an equivalent recognized rating tool.

**By level:**

- Silver: DOE Home Energy Score of 6
- Gold: DOE Home Energy Score of 8
- Platinum: DOE Home Energy Score of 10 (existing) — or 17.5 MBtu/year (new construction)

*Counts toward badges: ghi-zero-energy-certified, ghi-zero-carbon-certified, ghi-zero-energy-ready, foam-free*

Reference: https://standard.greenstarhomes.org/requirements/home-energy-score

## Properly Sized Cooling Equipment
*Required at: Silver level and above*

Cooling equipment sized per ACCA Manual J 8th Edition (or equivalent load calculation).

**Scope:**

All air-conditioning and heat-pump equipment installed for space cooling — central, mini-split, and packaged systems.

Reference: https://standard.greenstarhomes.org/requirements/properly-sized-cooling

## Refrigerant Charge Verification
*Required at: Silver level and above*

Refrigerant charge test completed on all AC and heat-pump equipment.

**Scope:**

All split-system AC and heat-pump equipment installed (factory-charged window/PTAC units excluded).

Reference: https://standard.greenstarhomes.org/requirements/refrigerant-charge-test

## All-Electric or Dual-Fuel Heating
*Required at: Gold level and above*

Space heating is all-electric or dual-fuel/hybrid. (At Platinum, all-electric only.)

**Scope:**

The primary space-heating system — central heat pump, ducted air handler, mini-split, or hydronic system.

*Counts toward badges: ghi-zero-energy-certified, ghi-zero-carbon-certified, ghi-zero-energy-ready, electrified-living, peak-load-shaver*

Reference: https://standard.greenstarhomes.org/requirements/heating-electric-or-dual-fuel

## All-Electric or Solar Water Heating
*Required at: Gold level and above*

Water heating is all-electric (heat pump preferred) or solar.

**Scope:**

The primary domestic hot-water heater serving the dwelling. Recirculation loops and point-of-use boosters are downstream of this and not constrained by this requirement.

*Counts toward badges: ghi-zero-energy-certified, ghi-zero-carbon-certified, ghi-zero-energy-ready, electrified-living, peak-load-shaver*

Reference: https://standard.greenstarhomes.org/requirements/water-heating-electric-or-solar

## All-Electric Cooking
*Required at: Gold level and above*

Cooking appliances are all-electric.

**Scope:**

All primary cooking appliances in the dwelling — cooktop, range, oven, wall oven, microwave-convection. Outdoor grills and supplementary appliances (toaster oven, slow cooker) are excluded.

*Counts toward badges: ghi-zero-energy-certified, ghi-zero-carbon-certified, ghi-zero-energy-ready, electrified-living*

Reference: https://standard.greenstarhomes.org/requirements/cooking-all-electric

## All-Electric Dryer
*Required at: Gold level and above*

Clothes dryer is all-electric (heat-pump dryer preferred).

**Scope:**

The primary clothes dryer in the dwelling.

*Counts toward badges: ghi-zero-energy-certified, ghi-zero-carbon-certified, ghi-zero-energy-ready, electrified-living, peak-load-shaver*

Reference: https://standard.greenstarhomes.org/requirements/dryer-all-electric

## Time-of-Use / Peak-Load Appliances
*Required at: Platinum level and above*

Major appliances support time-of-use scheduling and/or peak-load avoidance.

**Scope:**

Major-load appliances: HVAC, water heater, EV charger, electric dryer, dishwasher, and (where present) battery storage system.

*Counts toward badges: resilience, peak-load-shaver*

Reference: https://standard.greenstarhomes.org/requirements/time-of-use-appliances

## Solar- and EV-Ready Electrical Service
*Required at: Platinum level and above*

200-amp breaker box with capacity to add future solar PV and electric-vehicle chargers.

**Scope:**

The main electrical service panel and the conduit/wiring runs from panel to roof and panel to the EV-charging location.

*Counts toward badges: ghi-zero-energy-certified, ghi-zero-carbon-certified, ghi-zero-energy-ready, resilience, walkability, peak-load-shaver*

Reference: https://standard.greenstarhomes.org/requirements/solar-ev-ready-breaker
