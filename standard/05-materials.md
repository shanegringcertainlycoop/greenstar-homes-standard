# Pillar 5: Materials & Environmental Impact

Lower the embodied-carbon footprint of new construction, avoid hazardous legacy materials, and source certified wood and floor finishes.

## Lead Paint Assessment (Pre-1978 Homes)
*Required at: Certified level and above · existing buildings only*

Review and resolve any potential lead-paint issues. Document via the Lead Abatement acknowledgement template.

**Scope:**

Lead paint and lead in plumbing fittings in dwellings built before 1978 (the year the U.S. banned residential lead-based paint).

*Counts toward badges: sustainable-materials*

Reference: https://standard.greenstarhomes.org/requirements/lead-assessment

## Asbestos Mitigation
*Required at: Certified level and above · existing buildings only*

Mitigate any asbestos found, or document that none is present.

**Scope:**

Asbestos-containing materials in existing dwellings, typically built before the U.S. asbestos phase-out (1989-current, depending on product).

*Counts toward badges: sustainable-materials*

Reference: https://standard.greenstarhomes.org/requirements/asbestos-mitigation

## Knob-and-Tube Wiring Removed
*Required at: Certified level and above · existing buildings only*

No knob-and-tube wiring remains active in the home.

**Scope:**

Knob-and-tube electrical wiring in existing dwellings (common in U.S. homes built 1880-1940).

*Counts toward badges: reduced-emf*

Reference: https://standard.greenstarhomes.org/requirements/no-knob-and-tube

## Embodied Carbon Reduction
*Required at: Certified level and above · new construction only*

Reduction in upfront embodied carbon below the 154 kg CO₂e/m² baseline. Approved tools: Beam Estimator, EC3, PHPP (PH Ribbon). Alternative pathways available at Silver (no concrete, RECs, solar offset, small home).

**Scope:**

Upfront embodied carbon (A1-A5 life-cycle stages) of new construction or major addition. Existing structure being kept in place is exempt by definition (zero new embodied carbon).

**By level:**

- Certified: Embodied-carbon evaluation required
- Silver: 10% reduction from baseline
- Gold: 15% reduction from baseline
- Platinum: 30% reduction from baseline

*Counts toward badges: ghi-zero-carbon-certified, foam-free, sustainable-materials*

Reference: https://standard.greenstarhomes.org/requirements/embodied-carbon-reduction

## Building Maintenance Instructions
*Required at: Silver level and above*

Create written instructions documenting how to maintain the building, equipment, and finishes.

**Scope:**

Written documentation of how to maintain the building, equipment, and finishes — handed off to the homeowner or tenant at project completion.

*Counts toward badges: accessibility, resilience*

Reference: https://standard.greenstarhomes.org/requirements/maintenance-instructions

## Homeowner / Tenant Training
*Required at: Silver level and above*

Train the homeowner or tenant on how to operate and maintain the building.

**Scope:**

In-person walkthrough and training session with the homeowner or tenant at project handover.

*Counts toward badges: accessibility, resilience*

Reference: https://standard.greenstarhomes.org/requirements/homeowner-training

## Certified Wood (No Tropical Species)
*Required at: Gold level and above · new construction only*

New wood products are FSC or SFI certified. No tropical wood.

**Scope:**

All new wood products installed in the dwelling — framing, flooring, trim, cabinetry, decking, structural panels.

*Counts toward badges: sustainable-materials*

Reference: https://standard.greenstarhomes.org/requirements/certified-wood-no-tropical

## Floorscore or Green Label Plus Carpet
*Required at: Platinum level and above · new construction only*

Any new carpet is Floorscore or Green Label Plus certified.

**Scope:**

Any newly installed carpet in the dwelling. Existing carpet that is being kept in place is exempt.

*Counts toward badges: sustainable-materials*

Reference: https://standard.greenstarhomes.org/requirements/floorscore-carpet
