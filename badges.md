# Achievement badges

Optional recognitions any certified project can add by meeting additional criteria.

## GHI Zero Energy Certified
*Pillar: energy*

Home produces as much renewable energy on-site as it consumes annually.

Reference: https://standard.greenstarhomes.org/badges/ghi-zero-energy-certified

## GHI Zero Carbon Certified
*Pillar: energy*

Net-zero operational carbon emissions, accounting for both energy use and grid mix.

Reference: https://standard.greenstarhomes.org/badges/ghi-zero-carbon-certified

## GHI Zero Energy Ready Certified
*Pillar: energy*

Home is built efficient and wired so a future solar PV installation can take it to net-zero energy.

Reference: https://standard.greenstarhomes.org/badges/ghi-zero-energy-ready

## Zero Water Capable
*Pillar: water*

Home is plumbed and equipped to operate without municipal water inputs (rainwater + greywater + on-site treatment).

Reference: https://standard.greenstarhomes.org/badges/zero-water-capable

## Electrified Living
*Pillar: energy*

All major end uses — heating, cooling, hot water, cooking, drying — are electric.

Reference: https://standard.greenstarhomes.org/badges/electrified-living

## Healthier Home (Clean Living)
*Pillar: indoor-air-quality*

Project goes beyond baseline IAQ requirements with low-emission materials, advanced filtration, and active monitoring.

Reference: https://standard.greenstarhomes.org/badges/healthier-home

## Accessibility
*Pillar: materials*

Universal design features — zero-step entries, wider doorways, accessible bathrooms — supporting aging in place.

Reference: https://standard.greenstarhomes.org/badges/accessibility

## Reduced Electromagnetic Frequency (EMF)
*Pillar: indoor-air-quality*

Wiring, lighting, and wireless infrastructure designed to minimize EMF exposure for occupants.

Reference: https://standard.greenstarhomes.org/badges/reduced-emf

## Resilience
*Pillar: energy*

Backup power, passive survivability, hardened envelope, and other features that keep the home livable during extreme events.

Reference: https://standard.greenstarhomes.org/badges/resilience

## Foam-free
*Pillar: materials*

Built without spray-foam or rigid-foam insulation — uses fiber, cellulose, mineral wool, or other low-impact alternatives.

Reference: https://standard.greenstarhomes.org/badges/foam-free

## Home Water Score
*Pillar: water*

Project completes a formal Home Water Score assessment as supplemental water-performance documentation.

Reference: https://standard.greenstarhomes.org/badges/home-water-score

## Nature-friendly
*Pillar: moisture*

Site design supports pollinators, native ecology, dark skies, and habitat preservation.

Reference: https://standard.greenstarhomes.org/badges/nature-friendly

## Walkability
*Pillar: materials*

Project sited where occupants can reach daily destinations on foot, bike, or transit.

Reference: https://standard.greenstarhomes.org/badges/walkability

## Peak Load Shaver
*Pillar: energy*

Smart controls, storage, and demand response coordinate the home with grid peak-load signals.

Reference: https://standard.greenstarhomes.org/badges/peak-load-shaver

## Storm Water Control
*Pillar: moisture*

Site retains, infiltrates, or treats stormwater on-site — rain gardens, permeable surfaces, cisterns.

Reference: https://standard.greenstarhomes.org/badges/stormwater-control

## Sustainable Materials
*Pillar: materials*

Materials are reclaimed, biobased, third-party certified, or otherwise selected for low environmental impact.

Reference: https://standard.greenstarhomes.org/badges/sustainable-materials
