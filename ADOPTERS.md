# Adopters

Programs, organizations, and notable projects that use or build on the
GreenStar Homes standard. Using the standard — or forked it for your own
program? Add yourself with a pull request.

## Programs & organizations

- **Inner City Christian Federation (ICCF)** — Grand Rapids, MI. Adopted the
  standard as the renovation quality floor across a 213-unit affordable
  housing portfolio; 12 homes certified under GreenStar Renovation with
  before-and-after verification.
  [Case study](https://greenstarhomes.org/case-studies/iccf-community-homes)

## Notable certified projects

- **Vastu Shastra Sanctum** — Grand Rapids, MI. Silver; HERS −10 net-positive.
- **Capitol Park Apartments** — Lansing, MI. Silver; 48 units of LIHTC
  affordable housing.
- **Bradford Station** — Grand Rapids, MI. Michigan's first GreenStar
  multifamily certification; all-electric through Michigan winters.
- **1533 W Superior Street** — Chicago, IL. The first GreenStar multifamily
  certification anywhere (Manual v2).
- **Prickly Pear Sanctuary** — Grand Rapids, MI. Platinum; HERS 7.

More: https://greenstarhomes.org/case-studies
